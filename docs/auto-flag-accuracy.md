# Automatic flagging (Buy & Sell): accuracy report

Date: 2026-11-02. Checks: `app/screening.py` (prohibited item, gibberish title, spam).
Prohibited item: word list, then bge-small similarity to 21 prohibited descriptions and 19 harmless look-alikes. Production settings: similarity >= 0.6 and a lead of at least 0.0 over the best look-alike.
Test data: `tests/data/auto_flag_eval.csv` (630 listings) and `tests/data/spam_eval.json` (61 spam scenarios), generated by `scripts/generate_auto_flag_eval_data.py` (fixed seed).

## Limitations

- Synthetic titles from templates, labelled by the generator and not reviewed by a person. Real listings are messier.
- Only listing titles are checked here. Descriptions, Lost & Found reports and the photo check (NSFW model) are not in this report.
- Spam uses stubbed histories. The 24-hour window itself is enforced in SQL and is not tested here.
- The prohibited check is only as broad as its descriptions. Illegal items not described in `PROHIBITED` will be missed.
- The drug slang rules were written after seeing the `drugs_slang` group, so that group is optimistic. `drugs_slang_heldout` uses terms the rules did not include, and is the honest figure for slang.
